## Overview

Architected and built **Oxygen 2.0**, a luxury athletics and fitness web experience designed with a dark cyberpunk aesthetic, high-fps scroll animations, and interactive class booking.

## Engineering & Performance

- **Cinematic Interactions**: Implemented hardware-accelerated Framer Motion transitions and custom 3D card tilt effects.
- **Responsive Architecture**: Built mobile-first with Next.js App Router and optimized typography and layout shifts.
- **Interactive Tiers**: Integrated dynamic membership tier selection and interactive trainer showcase.
